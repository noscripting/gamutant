gamutant.css’s lab

Your accent, your page

This page asks nothing and runs no script. Its colors come from your system’s accent color , as the browser reports it to CSS as AccentColor, and gamutant.css’s parts build the rest from it, each reading the color the part around it wrote.

Secondary text: .gamut-readable-text at 4.5:1 on the paper. The text above is at 7:1, in the paper’s own shade. The paper is the accent’s hue at lightness 0.97 and 30% of the screen’s edge chroma (.gamut-screen-edge); in a dark scheme, 0.21 and 25%.

A panel. The accent’s hue at lightness 0.92 and 40% of the edge chroma, with its own ink at 7:1.
A button in your accent

The fill is the accent itself (.gamut-fit-screen keeps it on screen when the dark scheme lifts it); its label is the system’s own AccentColorText when that reaches 4.5:1, else white, else black (.gamut-contrast-color). The strip is .gamut-palette-step’s Material tones 95 to 10.

The controls keep accent-color: auto: the browser paints them in the same accent. Chrome reports a fixed #0075ff whatever the system’s accent; Safari and Firefox report the system’s.
