Relit

Each pixel of the photo becomes a reflectance curve over 38 wavelengths (spectral.js’s model: its seven base curves weighted by the pixel’s linear RGB), lit by the light picked below and seen again through CIE 1931’s colour matching functions, as a camera set for daylight or as an eye that adapts to the light (Bradford). The stylesheet works out every pixel, with no script; a temperature is a black body, Planck’s law evaluated in calc().
