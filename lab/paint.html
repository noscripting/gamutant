gamutant.css’s lab

Mixed as paint

Paints mix by what they absorb, so blue and yellow make green. In each pair the top row is gamutant.css’s .gamut-color-mix.gamut-mix-paint, spectral.js 3.0.0’s Kubelka–Munk model in calc(); the row under it is color-mix(in oklab), CSS’s own mix, which averages the colors in Oklab. No draft defines paint mixing (csswg-drafts#8715).

Three paints

gamutant.css mixes two paints. These triangles come from a sheet in its lab, not in gamutant.css: spectral.js’s mix() of three colors, one at each corner, every share in tenths. Its classes and properties are the lab’s own (.color-paint-3, --paint-*).
