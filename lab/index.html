gamutant.css’s lab

Pages from gamutant.css’s lab: the sheet’s gamut mapping and paint mixing shown beside other ways to compute the same colors, a theme built from the viewer’s accent color, and a photo relit by its reflectances. They are not part of the package (npm gets gamutant.css, parts/ and the docs); its checks are in test/.

  • Gamut mapping, four ways: .gamut-fit-screen with each --gamut-method (css, edge-seeker, ray-trace, adaptive) on 6 hues × 48 lightnesses at chroma 0.4, in sRGB and Display P3, beside each channel clipped and beside Color.js 0.7.1. css and ray-trace are within 0.84/255 of Color.js in all 1,152 cells (Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4, Firefox 159, 2026-10-31). Checked by test/gate-check.mjs (css), test/mappers-check.mjs (edge-seeker, ray-trace) and test/adaptive-check.mjs (adaptive).
  • Mixed as paint: .gamut-mix-paint beside color-mix(in oklab), and three paints from a lab sheet that gamutant.css doesn’t ship. Within 2.4e-5 a linear channel of spectral.js’s mix() in all 54 two-paint and 198 three-paint cells (Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4, Firefox 159, 2026-10-31). The two-paint mix is checked by test/paint-check.mjs.
  • Your accent: a page themed from your system’s AccentColor by gamutant.css’s parts nested (.gamut-screen-edge, .gamut-readable-text, .gamut-fit-screen, .gamut-contrast-color, .gamut-palette-step), and the same theme on macOS’s seven accents. All eight themes keep their ratios on 8-bit sRGB: ink at least 7.01:1, muted text 4.51:1, the button’s label 4.88:1 (Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4, Firefox 159, 2026-10-31).
  • Relit: a photo lit again under seven lights and seven black-body temperatures, every pixel a reflectance curve seen under the light, from a lab sheet that gamutant.css doesn’t ship. Every pixel within 1.6e-5 a channel of spectral.js’s model under all 7 lights and 7 temperatures, as camera and as eye (Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4, Firefox 159, 2026-10-31).
