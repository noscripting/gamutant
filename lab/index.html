gamutant.css’s lab

Pages from gamutant.css’s lab: the sheet’s gamut mapping and paint mixing shown beside other ways to compute the same colors. They are not part of the package (npm gets gamutant.css, parts/ and the docs); its checks are in test/.

  • Gamut mapping, four ways: .gamut-fit-screen with each --gamut-method (css, edge-seeker, ray-trace, adaptive) on 6 hues × 48 lightnesses at chroma 0.4, in sRGB and Display P3, beside each channel clipped and beside Color.js 0.7.1. css and ray-trace are within 0.84/255 of Color.js in all 1,152 cells (Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4, Firefox 159, 2026-10-31). Checked by test/gate-check.mjs (css), test/mappers-check.mjs (edge-seeker, ray-trace) and test/adaptive-check.mjs (adaptive).
  • Mixed as paint: .gamut-mix-paint beside color-mix(in oklab), and three paints from a lab sheet that gamutant.css doesn’t ship. Within 2.4e-5 a linear channel of spectral.js’s mix() in all 54 two-paint and 198 three-paint cells (Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4, Firefox 159, 2026-10-31). The two-paint mix is checked by test/paint-check.mjs.
