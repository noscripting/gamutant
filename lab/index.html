gamutant.css’s lab

Experiments from gamutant.css’s lab. They are not part of the npm package, and none runs a script.

  • Gamut mapping, four ways: .gamut-fit-screen with each of its four methods, beside Color.js.
  • Mixed as paint: .gamut-mix-paint beside color-mix(), and a mix of three paints.
  • Your accent: a page themed from your system’s accent color.
  • Relit: a photo lit again under seven lights and seven temperatures.

Computed by layout and fonts

Each of these writes a number as a width or a position and reads it back in the stylesheet, as Computing with CSS Layout explains. gamutant.css itself doesn’t work this way.

  • Typed color name: type a hex color and get the nearest CSS color name.
  • Eyedropper: hover a picture to read the color under the pointer.
  • Find a palette: the five dominant colors of an image by k-means, one round at a time until it settles.

The last two run in Chrome and Safari only: Firefox has no view timelines yet. Every page here was checked in Chrome 155, WebKit 26.4 and Firefox 159 on 2026-11-12.
