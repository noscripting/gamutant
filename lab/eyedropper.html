Eyedropper

Hover any pixel of the picture. Its color is read back by CSS: the swatch is rgb() of the three numbers read, and the hex and rgb() below it are counters. No script runs on this page. Each of the 24 bits of the color is a float whose shape-outside is that bit of the picture, masked to the hovered pixel; a view timeline reads the float's edge back as a number. Chrome and Safari; Firefox has no view timelines yet.

Viewing conditions and source
Sample background

IEC 61966-2-1:1999 (sRGB), as documented by ICC, specifies an image background of 16 cd/m² and display white of 80 cd/m² (D65). This page uses their 0.2 ratio as color(srgb-linear 0.2 0.2 0.2), encoded by the sRGB transfer function as approximately rgb(48.4529% 48.4529% 48.4529%).

The physical viewing surround is a separate quantity: ICC lists 4.1 cd/m² and ambient illumination of 64 lux. This page sets a relative background color only. Display luminance, black level, white point and room lighting are not measured or controlled here; this is not a calibrated reference viewing environment. White and black backgrounds are comparisons, not reference conditions.

Upstream WPT color tests check native sRGB-linear and XYZ-D65 rendering. The white interface and narrow sample frames are this project’s layout convention, not a WPT viewing standard. Spectral models have separate reference checks.
